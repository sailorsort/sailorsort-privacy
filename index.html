This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use and disclosure of Your information when You use the Sailor Sort mobile game (the Service) on Android or iOS, and tells You about Your privacy rights and how the law protects You.

In short: the Service has no account and no registration, We do not ask for Your name, email address or phone number, and Your game progress and settings are stored only on Your Device. The Service shows advertisements and offers optional in-app purchases. Our advertising partners and the app stores collect certain device and usage information, as described below. Where required by applicable law, We process Your information only where We have a valid legal basis to do so, including Your consent (where consent is required).

Collecting and Using Your Personal Information

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

The Service does not ask You to provide Us with information that can be used to contact or identify You directly. There is no registration form, no sign-in and no profile. We receive personally identifiable information only if You choose to contact Us, in which case We receive:

  • Your email address and the content of Your message (only when You email Us)

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when You use the Service, mainly by the advertising Service Providers integrated into the Application.

Usage Data may include information such as Your Device's Internet Protocol address (IP address), from which an approximate location (such as country or city) may be derived, Your Advertising Identifier, other device identifiers generated by the advertising software, and information about the advertisements You see and interact with (for example, impressions, clicks, video completions and rewards granted).

When You use the Service on a mobile device, Our Service Providers may collect certain information automatically, including, but not limited to, the type and model of mobile device You use, Your mobile operating system and its version, the language and time zone of Your Device, network type and carrier, screen size, the version of the Application, and other technical and diagnostic data needed to deliver advertisements.

The Company itself does not operate servers for the Service and does not use any analytics or crash reporting service. We do not receive this Usage Data in a form that identifies You; the advertising Service Providers may provide Us with aggregated reports, such as the number of advertisements shown and the resulting revenue.

Tracking Technologies and Cookies

The Application is not a website and does not use browser cookies. The advertising software integrated into the Application uses technologies that work in a similar way to identify Your Device and measure advertisements. The technologies used may include:

  • Advertising Identifier. Used to show ads, limit how often You see the same ad, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. On Android You can reset it or delete it in Your Device settings. On iOS it is accessed only if You allow tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.

  • SDK identifiers and local storage. The advertising software may store identifiers and settings on Your Device, including advertising settings.

  • Privacy Sandbox (Android). On supported Android devices, the advertising software may use Android's privacy-preserving advertising APIs for ad measurement and interest-based advertising.

Information Stored Only on Your Device

Your game progress (such as levels and coins), Your settings (such as sound, music and vibration) and records of Your purchases and rewards are saved only on Your Device. This information is not sent to Us, and We cannot access, restore or transfer it.

In-App Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed through Unity In-App Purchasing and Google Play Billing (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). Payments are handled entirely by Google or Apple under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see or store Your card number or other payment details. The Application receives only the information needed to deliver what You bought, such as the product purchased and a transaction confirmation. Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Information We Do Not Collect

The Service does not request access to, and does not collect, Your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files. The Service does not require an Account and does not collect Your name, phone number or postal address.

On Android, the Application requests the following permissions: internet access and network state (to load advertisements and process purchases), the advertising ID permission (to read the Advertising Identifier), the billing permission (for in-app purchases), vibration (for haptic feedback), and a small number of technical permissions required by the included software libraries, such as Android's advertising measurement permissions.

Advertising and Your Choices

The Service is free to play and is supported by advertisements. The Application does not currently show its own advertising consent form. Advertisements are served through Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads, which process data under their own privacy policies (linked above) and may show contextual or non-personalized advertisements. You can limit personalized advertising at any time with the advertising settings of Your Device, as described below.

  • Android: You can reset or delete Your Advertising Identifier in Your Device settings (for example Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on Your Device).

  • iOS: the Application accesses the IDFA only if You allow it in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

If You decline personalized advertising, You may still see advertisements, but they will be less relevant to You (contextual or non-personalized advertisements). Ads are shown on platforms where advertising is enabled for the Service; advertising availability may differ between Android and iOS.
