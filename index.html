Privacy Policy - Sailor Sort

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GameSaSa ("we", "us") handles information when you play the mobile game Sailor Sort (the "Game") on Android and iOS.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account. The Game does not require registration.

Your game progress and settings (such as levels, coins, sound and music options) are stored only on your device.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The Game uses third-party services that may collect information automatically, such as device model, operating system, language, IP address, advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / Apple IDFA), and how you interact with ads:

  • Advertising: Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and its ad network partners - privacy policy
  • In-app purchases: Google Play Billing / Apple App Store, via Unity IAP - payments are handled by Google or Apple; we never see your card details. Google, Apple

This information is used to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and process purchases.

3. Personalized ads and your choices

Ads may be personalized based on your advertising ID. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK or some US states), we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

4. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help delete it.

5. Data retention and deletion

Game data on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game. For data held by the third-party services above, please refer to their privacy policies, or contact us and we will forward your request.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
